❓ Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about $REBL security

Is the contract safe?

Yes, the contract is verified, mint and freeze authorities are renounced, and 98.57% of the LP is permanently locked — unclaimable since launch.

How do I verify the contract?

Use the verification links above or manually check on Solscan and RugCheck.

Is there a rug pull risk?

No. 98.57% of the LP is permanently locked and can never be withdrawn — it was minted directly to the pump.fun protocol at launch and is unclaimable by design. With mint and freeze authorities also renounced, there is no rug pull vector.

Can the contract be changed?

No, mint and freeze authorities are renounced. The contract is immutable.

What about team tokens?

Team, ecosystem, and ZORA Rewards Treasury tokens are locked in transparent vesting schedules with cliffs, verifiable on Streamflow.

How do I report a security issue?

Reach out through our official community channels on Telegram or Discord. Never share private keys or seed phrases.
