Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about $rebelinux

What is the total supply of $rebelinux?

Fixed at 1,000,000,000 (1 billion) $rebelinux tokens on the Base network.

How are rewards distributed?

Contest winners earn $rebelinux from the Protocol Reserves. Winners also receive a free mint of the chapter's Zora collectible. See contests →

How does the Ecosystem Flow work?

All ZORA creator fees are directly reinvested into the creation of new historical chapters and the funding of community contests.

What is the founder's vesting schedule?

The creator coin on Zora has a standard 50/50 split. The creator does not have a separate vesting schedule beyond their public allocation.

How can I verify the token?

All transactions and holdings are verifiable on Zora (Base network). You can view the collection at zora.co/@rebelinux.
