Frequently Asked Questions

What is the total supply of $REBL?

Fixed at 1,000,000,000 (1 billion) $REBL tokens, with deflationary pressure through buy-back and burn.

How are rewards distributed?

The ZORA Rewards Treasury (170M $REBL, 17%) funds contest prizes and volume-milestone bonuses. Winners earn $REBL for participating in Thumbnail, Tokenization, or Journey contests. See contests →

How does Value Acceleration work?

All ZORA creator fees flow into the Value Acceleration mechanism: 50% buy-back & burn, 20% AI production, 15% reserve, 15% operations. Deployments are triggered by REBL Governance Hub vote.

What is the founder's vesting schedule?

Founder-held tokens are subject to a 3-year linear vesting schedule with a 40% cliff, demonstrating long-term alignment with the community.

What replaced the epoch reward system?

In v3.0, rewards shifted from holding-based (epochs, age bonuses, Rebel Key) to participation-based. The community now earns $REBL through contests — designing thumbnails, selecting scenes to tokenize, and voting on the next Journey.

How can I verify vesting contracts?

All vesting contracts are publicly verifiable on Streamflow and Solscan. Links are provided for each allocation above.
