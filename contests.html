⚙️ The Process

How a Contest Works

From submission to reward — 5 steps

1

Contest Opens

Announced on X. The challenge, reward, and deadline are specified.

2

You Submit

Post your entry on X with your Zora wallet address. Free to enter. No purchase required.

3

Community Votes on X

All entries are published. Anyone can vote for their preferred entry through a poll on X.

4

Winner Announced

The entry with the most votes wins. In case of a tie, the project owner decides.

5

Reward & Production

The winner receives their $rebelinux reward and a free mint. Tokenization winners see their scene become a content coin on Zora.
