How a Contest Works

From submission to reward — 6 steps

1

Contest Opens

Announced on X and in the Governance Hub. The challenge, reward, and deadline are specified.

2

You Submit

Post your entry on X with your Solana wallet address. Free to enter. No purchase required.

3

Hub Pre-Selects Finalists

The REBL Governance Hub (500K+ $REBL) reviews entries and shortlists finalists based on skill criteria.

4

Community Votes on X

Finalists are published. Anyone can vote for their preferred entry through a poll on X.

5

Winner Announced

The finalist with the most votes wins. In case of a tie, the project owner decides. A 75% Hub supermajority may veto in extreme cases.

6

Reward & Production

The winner receives their $REBL reward. Tokenization Contest winners see their scene become a content coin on Zora, with volume bonuses unlocking as it trades.
