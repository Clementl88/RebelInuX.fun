THE JOURNEY CONTEST

Community Decides the Next Journey

The Journey Contest determines which historical period and country will be the next Journey collection. The Hub pre-selects finalists. The community votes on X.

How It Works

  • Submit: Create an original image featuring the RebelInuX mascot in a specific country and historical period
  • Pre-select: The Governance Hub reviews all entries and shortlists finalists
  • Vote: The community votes on X among the finalists
  • Win: The finalist with the most votes wins
  • Produce: The project creates AI-animated Journey Collectibles based on the winning theme

Selection Criteria

Hub Pre-Selection: The REBL Governance Hub selects finalists based on creative vision, historical significance, and alignment with the RebelInuX mascot's identity.

Public Vote: Once finalists are published, anyone can vote for their preferred entry through a poll on X.

Tie-Breaking: In the rare event of a tie, the project owner makes the final decision.

Veto: In extreme cases (offensive, illegal, or plagiarized content), the Governance Hub may veto a winner by a 75% supermajority vote.

The Prize

The winning theme becomes the project's next official Journey. Winner receives:

  • Base $REBL reward (announced per contest)
  • Public credit as contest winner
  • $rebelinux Creator Coins
  • Free mint of the first video collectible from the collection

Reward Flow

The newly minted Journey collection becomes available as content coins on Zora. The community can trade them, and the winning scene becomes a permanent part of the historical journey.

A new contest begins once the current Journey is completed. Duration varies by historical theme.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I join the REBL Governance Hub?

Hold at least 500,000 $REBL tokens in your wallet, then use the Collab.Land verification link to connect your wallet (signature only, no private keys). You'll be automatically verified and granted access to the private Telegram group.

Why 1 member = 1 vote instead of token-weighted?

Equal voting prevents whale dominance and ensures every qualified participant has the same say. This creates true community governance where ideas matter more than wallet size.

How does the Journey Contest work?

The REBL Governance Hub pre-selects a shortlist of finalists based on creative vision, historical significance, and alignment with the mascot's identity. The community then votes for the winner on X among the finalists. In case of a tie, the project owner decides. A 75% supermajority of the Hub may veto in extreme cases (offensive, illegal, or plagiarized content).

What is the ZORA Rewards Treasury?

The ZORA Rewards Treasury (170M $REBL, 17%) funds contest prizes and volume-milestone bonuses. 40% (68M) is released as a cliff, and 60% (102M) vests linearly over 2 years. The treasury is secured in a multi-sig cold storage wallet.

Is Collab.Land verification safe?

Yes! Collab.Land is non-custodial and only requires a wallet signature (not a transaction). No private keys or seed phrases are ever required. It's read-only access used by thousands of reputable Web3 communities.

When will the on-chain DAO launch?

If the community grows to demand a full on-chain DAO, a vote within the Governance Hub will initiate that transition. Until then, the REBL Governance Hub remains the definitive decision-making body.
