❓ Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the gallery

What is this gallery for?

This gallery showcases community artwork and past contest winners. To see the AI-animated Journey Collectibles themselves, visit the Journey page.

How can I enter a contest?

Go to the Contests page to see the active contest, the rules, and how to submit. Contests are free to enter. Winners earn $REBL.

What kinds of contests exist?

Three main types: Thumbnail Contests (design a video thumbnail), Tokenization Contests (choose a scene to become a content coin on Zora), and Journey Contests (propose the next historical theme). Full rules on the Contests page.

How do I submit my artwork?

Share your artwork on X, Discord, or submit it directly to an active contest. Include your social handle and Solana wallet address for credit and reward distribution.

Can I use the RebelInuX brand assets?

Yes — for fan art, community projects, and non-commercial purposes. Do not use the branding for scam projects, misleading promotions, or unauthorized commercial activities. Please credit RebelInuX and link to our official channels. Logo design by Masum B.
