🛡️ Guidelines

Community Guidelines

To maintain a positive and productive environment for all rebels

Our Community Values

To maintain a positive and productive environment for all rebels, we ask everyone to follow these simple guidelines.

Respect Everyone

Treat all community members with respect, regardless of experience level or background.

No Spam or Scams

Keep discussions constructive. No spam, scam links, or shilling other projects.

Help New Rebels

Welcome newcomers and help them understand the ecosystem. We grow together.

Constructive Criticism

Feedback is welcome, but keep it constructive and helpful to the community.

Stay Positive

Keep discussions positive. Unsubstantiated fear and toxicity hurt community growth.

Stay On Topic

Keep discussions relevant to RebelInuX and the broader crypto ecosystem.

Violation of guidelines may result in removal from community channels.

❓ Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the community

How do I join the community?

Join our Telegram group, follow us on X, join our Discord server, or follow us on ZORA using the links above!

How do I participate in a contest?

Visit the Contests page. Each contest specifies the steps — you submit an entry and post it on X with your Solana wallet address. Contests are free to enter.

Which platform is most active?

Telegram is our most active chat platform, while X is best for announcements and contest voting.

Are you on TikTok and Instagram?

Yes! Follow us on TikTok (@rebelinux) and Instagram (@rebelinux_rebl) for visual content, Journey Collectible previews, and community highlights.

Do I need to hold tokens to enter a contest?

No. Contests are free to enter. You only need a Solana wallet address to receive your reward if you win.

How can I contribute?

Share ideas, create content, help newcomers, participate in contests, and engage in discussions across our platforms.
