🏛️ The Concept

What is a Journey?

A collection of AI-animated historical scenes, owned by the community

Real Paintings, Not Inventions

We start with the original historical painting. Every figure, every building, every landscape comes from the source. Nothing is invented.

AI-Animated, Faithfully

We use AI to animate the painting while staying close to the original style, colors, composition, and the painter's intent.

Our Mascot, In Every Scene

RebelInuX appears within the scene, painting the historical moment on a canvas. The mascot is the artist, the witness, the storyteller.

Selected by the Community

Each Journey is a collection of scenes. The community chooses which scenes become tokenized content coins on Zora.

About Journey Collectibles

Journey Collectibles are AI-generated videos retracing important historical events. Each Journey is a collection of multiple video Collectibles. The subject of each Journey is determined by the REBL Governance Hub through the Journey Contest (Hub pre-selection + community vote on X).

Free to watch. Viewing is not gated by token ownership. The tokenized collectibles represent ownership of a specific scene — but anyone can watch the source videos.

Learn about contests →
🎥 Formats

Two Video Formats

Short-form vertical + long-form YouTube

Short-Form Vertical Videos

Each chapter and each individual part is released as a short vertical video — up to 2 minutes — on TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram, and Facebook. The mascot appears within the scene.

TikTok YouTube Instagram Facebook

Long-Form YouTube Videos

When a Journey is complete, the full story is assembled into a single long-form video on YouTube. Every chapter in order, from opening to final part, in one continuous narrative.

⚙️ The Process

How Scenes Become Collectibles

From painting to content coin — 6 steps

1

Paintings Selected

A set of historical paintings is chosen for a chapter.

2

Animated by AI

Each painting is animated and woven into the video narrative.

3

Published Everywhere

The video is released across all RebelInuX channels.

4

Community Chooses

A Tokenization Contest selects which scene becomes a content coin.

5

Minted on Zora

The winning scene becomes a tradeable content coin on Zora.

6

Fees Flow Back

Trading fees flow into the Value Acceleration mechanism (50% buy-back & burn / 20% AI / 15% reserve / 15% ops).
