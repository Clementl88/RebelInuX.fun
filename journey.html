The Craft

Real Paintings. Animated.

We don't invent history — we make it move.

01

Original Paintings

Every scene starts from a real historical painting. Nothing is invented — the figures, buildings, and light come from the source.

02

AI Animation

We animate the painting with AI while staying close to the original style, colors, composition, and the painter's intent.

03

The Mascot

RebelInuX appears inside every scene, painting the moment on a canvas. The mascot is the artist, the witness, the storyteller.

04

Community Selects

Each chapter's tokenized scene is chosen by the community through Tokenization Contests. The rebellion decides what lives on-chain.
