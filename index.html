Time-Traveling Art,
Collectively Preserved

Our mascot travels through history, painting scenes as they unfold. We take real historical paintings and animate them with AI — transforming static artwork into living, breathing video collectibles.

Currently witnessing the 1789 French Revolution. But this is only the beginning. Future Journeys will explore historical events from every country and every century.

This is a collective work. The community decides our next destinations, which scenes matter, and what our mascot wears in each era.
