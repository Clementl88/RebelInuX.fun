🏆 Tokenization Rewards

Cumulative Volume Bonuses up to $111

Every time your tokenized scene reaches a new volume milestone, the bonus stacks. Cumulative — reaching $50,000 pays all five levels.

$2,500 +$3 → $3 total
$5,000 +$6 → $9 total
$10,000 +$12 → $21 total
$25,000 +$30 → $51 total
$50,000 +$60 → $111 total

A base $REBL reward (announced per contest) is added on top. Volume bonuses apply to Tokenization Contests only.
