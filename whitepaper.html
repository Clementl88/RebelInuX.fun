2-Asset Baseline + Boosters NEW

$rebelinux Creator Coin + Permanent Rebel Key form the base eligibility. Journey Collectibles on ZORA (Base) are optional yield boosters that multiply your rewards.

Age-Weighted Rewards

+7% per epoch per batch. At 20 epochs, the batch reaches +140% weight. No staking required — just hold.

Governance-Triggered Value Acceleration

ZORA creator fees are split into four governance-controlled pools: 50% for buy-back and burn, 20% for AI production, 15% for reserve, 15% for operations. All deployments are triggered by REBL Governance Hub vote.

Collector Bonuses NEW

+0.05x for every collectible you hold. +0.1x for being the largest holder of any collectible. The more you collect, the more you earn.

REBL Governance Hub

Token-gated Telegram via Collab.land. 500K $REBL threshold, 1 member = 1 vote. Controls Journey Contest winners and protocol parameters.

Journey Contest

Community members vote on next historical theme (60% vote + 40% engagement). Winner receives public credit, crypto rewards, $rebelinux Creator Coins, and free mint.
