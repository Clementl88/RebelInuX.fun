Key Whitepaper v3.0 Information

Participation Rewards NEW

The system rewards participation, not holding. Enter contests by designing thumbnails, proposing scenes to tokenize, or suggesting the next Journey theme. Winners earn $REBL.

Base Reward + Volume Bonuses

Every contest winner receives a base $REBL reward (amount announced per contest). If the tokenized scene reaches trading volume milestones, cumulative bonuses apply — up to $111 total.

Governance-Triggered Value Acceleration

ZORA creator fees are split into four governance-controlled pools: 50% buy-back & burn, 20% AI production, 15% reserve, 15% operations. All deployments are triggered by REBL Governance Hub vote.

Three Contest Types NEW

Thumbnail Contests, Tokenisation Contests, and Journey Contests. Governance Hub pre-selects finalists; the community then votes for the winner on X.

REBL Governance Hub

Token-gated Telegram via Collab.land. 500K $REBL threshold, 1 member = 1 vote. Pre-selects contest finalists, votes on treasury proposals and protocol parameters, and ratifies negotiated partnerships.

Rebel Journey Collectibles

AI-animated historical scenes retracing events like the French Revolution. Each scene selected through a contest becomes a content coin on Zora, tradeable and collectible.

Frequently Asked Questions

What version is this whitepaper?

This is version 3.0, published October 2026. It covers the participation-based SocialFi ecosystem, contest rewards, Journey Collectibles as content coins, and the revised tokenomics.

What's new in v3.0?

New features include: participation-based rewards via contests, base reward + cumulative volume bonuses for winners, three contest types (Thumbnail, Tokenisation, Journey), Governance Hub pre-selection + community vote on X, and per-scene Rebel Journey Collectibles from Chapter V onward.

How do I earn $REBL rewards?

Enter contests. Winners earn a base $REBL reward plus cumulative volume bonuses if their tokenized scene reaches trading milestones. Contests are free to enter — no purchase is required.

How are winners selected?

The REBL Governance Hub reviews all submissions and pre-selects a shortlist of finalists based on skill criteria. The community then votes for the winner on X. In the rare event of a tie, the project owner makes the final decision.
